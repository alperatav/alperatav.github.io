01 My story

Learning, building,
and aiming high.

I study Information Systems and Technologies at Istanbul Okan University.

Classes can be challenging sometimes, but I keep working through them. My strongest side has always been entrepreneurship. Ever since I was a kid, I’ve wanted to build something meaningful—and preferably something a little bigger than my current comfort zone.

At 18, I worked on my first startup project. That experience gave me the chance to speak with foundations about the idea and even connect with a product manager at Google. It was not a partnership or endorsement—just a valuable conversation that showed me how far curiosity and initiative can take you.

That first project did not succeed, but it gave me something more useful than a perfect ending: experience. I learned that a failed attempt is not the opposite of progress. Sometimes it is the progress—just wearing less flattering clothes.

Alper’s Story

A game inspired by a real-life love story. Alper tries every route he can think of to find love, but each path leads somewhere other than romance. There are different methods, different choices, and—so far—the same stubborn ending.

The plan: test the demo in Türkiye first, then prepare it for an American release. Romance is the final boss, and Alper is still grinding.

Atav Tasks

A simple AI-powered tool that finds action items hidden inside messy PDFs and images, then turns them into clear task cards.

The idea: spend less time hunting for assignments and more time actually doing them—because nobody should have to play “Where’s Waldo?” with a deadline.
